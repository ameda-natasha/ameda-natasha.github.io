Web vulnerabilities are common entry point for attackers, In this post we'll explore **sql-Injection** and **xss** -- two of the OWASP top 10.

##SQL Injection
This occurs when unsanitized inputs is used in database queries.

##XSS
Allows an attacker to inject scripts into web pages viewed by others.
